About Me

I am an Electrical & Electronic Engineer currently serving as Deputy Director and Laboratory In-charge at WHAP R&I, Walton Hi-Tech Industries PLC. I am passionate about building high-quality, compliant, and commercially competitive products through structured engineering processes, innovation, and strong teamwork.
